01

The Problem

Traditional golf putters suffer from a fundamental flaw:

🌀

Face Rotation

During the stroke, traditional putter heads rotate on their toe-heel axis, causing inconsistent face angle at impact.

📐

Torsional Flex

The head flexes around the hosel, creating energy loss and unpredictable ball roll.

🎯

Misalignment

Inertia coupling causes the putter to twist during backstroke, making alignment training difficult.

❌

The Result

Golfers spend hours practicing stroke mechanics when the tool itself fights against consistent ball-to-line transfer.

Market Opportunity

Zero torque putters have emerged as a dominant category in premium golf equipment. Major brands like Scotty Cameron and TaylorMade have invested heavily in torsion-resistant designs.

Our opportunity: Bring pro-grade zero torque technology to value-conscious enthusiasts through innovative manufacturing.

02

Market Research

Competitive Landscape

Brand Tech Price Availability
Scotty Cameron Bone Handle / Dual Hosel $450+ Limited
TaylorMade Spider Aluminum Insert / Weight Ports $300-$400 Wider
Odyssey White Hot OG Ceramic Core / Zero Torsion $125-$175 Easy

Key Insight: Premium zero torque technology is locked behind high price points and limited availability. There's room for innovation at the value segment.

Target Customer Requirements

  • Performance: Professional-grade torsional stability without premium pricing
  • Aesthetics: Clean, modern design that doesn't compromise on look
  • Value: $175-$275 price point with tangible quality advantages
  • Availability: Easy to source components and assemble

Design brief: Deliver Scotty Cameron-level torsion resistance at Odyssey pricing.

03

Engineering Requirements

Performance Specifications

Moment of Inertia (MOI) ≥2850 g·cm²
Torsional Rigidity ≤0.5° twist at impact
Center of Gravity (CG) 18-24mm behind face

Manufacturing Requirements

Tolerance Stack-up ±0.1mm critical dimensions
Surface Finish Ra ≤ 3.2 μm (machined)
Machinability 60-75 HBR aluminum alloy

Material Selection

🏗️ 6061-T6 Aluminum

Machinability, strength-to-weight ratio

🎨 Anodizing (Type III)

Hard coat, scratch resistant finish

04

CAD Development

Onshape Workflow

Our design process leveraged cloud-native CAD for collaboration and version control:

1

Baseline Geometry

Started with proven blade profile from existing zero-torque designs. Parametrically defined to enable quick iteration.

2

Torsion Optimization

Added stiffening ribs, mass redistribution, and hosel geometry variations. Used FEA to identify critical flex modes.

3

Tolerance Analysis

Defined GD&T callouts on all critical surfaces. Built assembly model with manufacturing tolerances baked in.

05

Design for Manufacturing

Machinability Considerations

  • ✅ All internal corners have ≥6° radius for tool access
  • ✅ No tight features - all holes ≥Φ4mm
  • ✅ Parting line positioned to minimize machining time

Tolerancing Strategy

Critical surfaces: ±0.05mm (face flatness, sole plane)
Secondary surfaces: ±0.1mm
Hole features: H7/g6 fits where applicable

Drawing Package

GD&T callouts and machining notes on production drawings

  • ✅ 3-view orthographic projection
  • ✅ Datum features defined (A, B, C)
  • ✅ Surface finish symbols throughout

Project Summary

The Zero Torque Golf Putter project demonstrates end-to-end product development capability:

  1. Market Research: Identified opportunity in value segment
  2. Engineering: Defined performance specs and manufacturability criteria
  3. CAD Development: Iterative design validated through simulation
  4. DFM: Designed for production from day one
  5. Production: Leveraged Xometry platform for quality machining

This project serves as a flagship example of taking a product from concept through to CNC-machined production — bridging mechanical engineering with modern manufacturing platforms.
